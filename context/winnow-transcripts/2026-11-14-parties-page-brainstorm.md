- **What goes on the page beyond venue, bands and 2-for-1 times?** Live
  on-now / up-next / 2-for-1 badges from the conference clock, band set times,
  getting there and back (map, travel time, last shuttle), entry notes and an
  Add to calendar link.
- **Which parties get bands and a 2-for-1 window?** Every one of the four.
- **Can attendees add a party to their agenda?** No — info only.
