# Turn the Home page into a welcome to the conference and its venues

## What this changes
Home stops repeating the navigation. The five quick-link tiles go. The slim hero bar becomes a larger banner whose background is generated, text-free art of Aurora and The Foundry (via `HeroMedia`; gradient when no image loads). Below it: an "About" blurb (name, edition, dates, city from `conference`, what ORBIT '26 is, two venues 6.2 miles apart), a "Getting there" section (each venue's address and opening hours, then the shuttle / rideshare / taxi minutes and cost between the sites in both directions, and a link to `/venues`), then the latest announcement and the sponsor marquee, unchanged. Nothing on it is about the signed-in attendee.

## Where
- `src/pages/HomePage.jsx` — delete `LINKS` and `QuickLinks`; enlarge `HeroBar` (taller, keep name/dates/city, the day chip and the two buttons); add `About` and `GettingThere` components, each wrapped in `<Reveal>` in `HomePage`. Venues and `travel` come from `useConference()`; use `routesBetween(travel, a.id, b.id)` from `src/lib/travel.js`, drop the `Walk` mode, show `minutes` and `costUsd` (0 shown as "Free"). Hours use `fmtTime` from `src/lib/format.js` on `opensAt`/`closesAt`. The "6.2 miles" figure is the one number the ticket states and the seed description carries; there is no field for it, so it is written in the blurb.
- `public/images/hero-1.jpg`, `hero-2.jpg` — generated, text-free, ~2:1, subject right of centre, under 400KB each (Aurora: convention campus at dusk; Foundry: decommissioned steel works). Produced in the build (e.g. a script rendering generated art to JPG), not photographs.
- `tests/home.spec.js` — replace the first test (five tiles) and the second's `quick-links` visibility check; add tests below. Uses `visit(page, '/')`, `conferenceDays()`/`API` from `tests/helpers.js`; read-only, so no data lane needed.
- `CLAUDE.md` § Deliberately unbuilt (Home bullet and the Following bullet's "Home" mention stay accurate), `.claude/rules/ui.md` "Home and My Agenda are blank on purpose" paragraph (it lists links), and `context/engineering-context/ui.md` `HomePage` section plus the line ~329 "A link on the home page" gotcha.

## Decisions
- Show only Shuttle, Rideshare and Taxi (omit Walk, 96 minutes) in the summary — OK?
- Show both directions of travel (Aurora→Foundry and Foundry→Aurora), since they differ by minutes — OK?
- Keep the "Day n · time" chip and the Browse today / Speakers buttons in the new hero, since the ticket does not remove them — OK?
- Hero art is generated by a script in the build and committed as JPGs, rather than fetched from an image service — OK?
- Write "6.2 miles" in the blurb as text, since no data field carries it — OK?

## Out of scope
Airport/hotel arrival, external map links, stat tiles, anything personal, things to do nearby.
